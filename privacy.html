Privacy Policy | Romex Watch Nepal

At Romex Watch Nepal, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data, including the use of tracking technologies like Facebook Pixel and Conversions API (CAPI).

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: Name, email, phone number, and delivery address provided when you place an order.
  • Payment Details: Payment or QR details are handled through the payment method agreed with you during the WhatsApp order conversation. ROMEX does not request or store your payment login credentials.
  • Browsing Behavior: Interactions with our online store, including pages viewed, products added to cart, and completed purchases.
  • Tracking Data: Information collected through tracking tools such as Facebook Pixel, Facebook Conversions API (CAPI), and other analytics tools.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Order Fulfillment: To process, package, and deliver your Romex timepieces across Nepal.
  • Customer Support: To communicate about your purchases, shipping updates, warranties, and queries.
  • Marketing Optimization: To measure the effectiveness of our advertisements, improve targeting, and provide relevant offers through platforms like Meta (Facebook, Instagram) and other advertising networks.
  • Platform Improvement: To analyze website traffic and user behavior to improve our storefront and services.

3. Sharing of Information

We never sell your data. Information may be shared only with:

  • Payment Processors: Payment providers or bank/wallet services used according to the payment method agreed with you in WhatsApp.
  • Shipping Partners: Regional logistics and courier services strictly for delivery purposes.
  • Advertising & Analytics Platforms: Providers like Meta and Google Analytics, strictly for marketing optimization and performance tracking.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and tracking technologies such as Facebook Pixel and CAPI to:

  • Understand visitor behavior and preferences on our website.
  • Measure the performance of our watch ads, Facebook Pixel, and CAPI.
  • Deliver more relevant content, discounts, and product offers to you.
  • Note: You can disable cookies in your browser settings or adjust your personalized ad preferences directly on Facebook and Google platforms.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard physical and electronic security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse.

6. Your Rights

  • You may request access, correction, or permanent deletion of your data at any time.
  • You may opt-out of marketing emails, newsletters, or tracking tools.
  • Contact us at romexwatchnepal@gmail.com for any privacy-related requests.

7. Updates

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect new tools, regulations, or business practices. Any changes will be updated directly on this page.
