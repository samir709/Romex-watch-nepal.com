Warranty, Returns & Refund Policy | Romex Watch Nepal

Your satisfaction is our priority. We want you to wear your Romex watch with absolute confidence.

  • Return & Exchange Window: We accept returns or exchange requests within 7 days of delivery. To be eligible, the watch must be unworn, unused, with all original tags attached, and in its original packaging.
  • Damaged or Incorrect Items: If you receive a damaged watch or the wrong model, please contact us immediately within 24 hours of delivery with a photo or unboxing video. We will arrange a free replacement.
  • Warranty Coverage: Every Romex watch purchased from our website comes with a 2-Year Warranty. The warranty is limited to battery coverage and water-resistance coverage only.
  • Warranty Exclusions: The warranty is limited to the battery and water-resistance coverage stated above. It does not cover normal wear and tear, scratches, glass or case damage, accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized repairs, or other issues outside battery and water-resistance coverage.
  • Refunds: Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will process your refund. Approved refunds will be arranged directly with the customer through the payment method agreed during the WhatsApp order conversation.
