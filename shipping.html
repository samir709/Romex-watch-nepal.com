Shipping & Delivery Policy | Romex Watch Nepal

All orders are processed and shipped directly from our central hub located in Mechinagar-6, Kakarvitta, Jhapa . We aim to deliver your favorite Romex timepieces safely and swiftly to your doorstep anywhere in Nepal.

Delivery Locations & Estimated Timeframes

  • Local Delivery (Mechinagar & Kakarvitta area): Same day or within 24 hours.
  • Within Jhapa District & Eastern Region (Birtamode, Damak, Itahari, Biratnagar, etc.): Delivery takes 1 to 2 business days.
  • Kathmandu Valley: Delivery takes 2 to 3 business days.
  • Rest of Nepal (Major cities and towns across all provinces): Delivery takes 3 to 5 business days, depending on your location and courier logistics.

Shipping Charges

  • Within Jhapa District: Flat fee of Rs. 200.
  • Outside Jhapa (Rest of Nepal): A maximum delivery fee of Rs. 220 applies, calculated at checkout based on your specific location.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

COD is available for most locations across Nepal. Please ensure someone is available at the provided delivery address to receive the order and make the cash payment to the courier.

Order Tracking

Once your watch is dispatched from Kakarvitta, we will send you a confirmation message or tracking details via SMS/WhatsApp so you can monitor your shipment.

Self-Pickup

Local customers living near Mechinagar-6, Kakarvitta can also choose to pick up their orders directly from our location. Please contact us before visiting to ensure your model is packaged and ready.
