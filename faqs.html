Frequently Asked Questions

1. Delivery & Orders

  • Where do you ship from?
    All orders are packaged and dispatched directly from our hub in Mechinagar-6, Kakarvitta, Jhapa.
  • How long will it take to receive my watch?
    Mechinagar/Kakarvitta: Same day or within 24 hours.
    Within Jhapa District: 1 to 2 business days.
    Kathmandu Valley: 2 to 3 business days.
    Rest of Nepal: 3 to 5 business days.
  • What are the shipping charges?
    Delivery is Rs. 200 within Jhapa. For any location outside of Jhapa, the maximum shipping fee is capped at Rs. 220.
  • Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
    Yes! Order details, payment instructions and QR/payment information are handled directly through our WhatsApp chat after we confirm your watch and delivery details.

2. Product & Quality

  • Are Romex watches water-resistant?
    Romex watches feature basic, everyday water resistance. They can easily withstand minor accidental splashes or light rain, but they are not designed for swimming, diving, or hot showers.
  • What kind of movement do these watches use?
    Our timepieces operate on highly reliable, low-maintenance Quartz (battery-powered) movements to ensure accurate timekeeping for your daily hustle.
  • How long does the battery last?
    The pre-installed battery typically lasts between 1 to 2 years depending on usage. The battery can easily be replaced at any local watch repair shop when it runs out.

3. Warranty & Returns

  • Do Romex watches come with a warranty?
    Yes! Every ROMEX watch comes with a 2-Year Warranty limited to battery coverage and water-resistance coverage.
  • Can I return or exchange my watch if I change my mind?
    We want you to love your purchase. We accept returns and exchange requests within 7 days of delivery, provided the watch is completely unworn, unused, and remains in its original packaging with all tags attached.
